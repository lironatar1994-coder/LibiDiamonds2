## Scope and mode

Homepage, Persuade. Help a first-time Israeli jewelry shopper understand the offer, enter a collection, and move toward virtual try-on or personal guidance.

## Audience, job and action

The visitor is choosing a lab-grown diamond jewel, often on mobile. Jewelry photography must lead; the primary action is collection discovery, followed by trying a piece on and requesting guidance.

## Proof and constraints

Use only the existing catalog, prices, service facts, guides and photography. Preserve Hebrew RTL, mobile clarity, the LIBI logo, real product imagery and the no-live-deployment boundary.

## Direction and memorable moment

Luminous Atelier Folio: a diamond-white split hero and flat photographic mosaic, interrupted once by the midnight virtual try-on passage before returning to a light atelier story.

None for this implementation.
